## Who we are

Yalla Gym ("we", "the platform") is operated by [OWNER: registered legal name of the company], [OWNER: registered address and country]. We decide how the personal data described here is used. You can reach us about your data at [OWNER: contact email address].

This policy explains what we collect when you use the Yalla Gym app and website, why, who receives it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it. It applies to clients, trainers and agents.

## The short version

- We collect what the app needs to plan, track and coach your training and nutrition, to run your account, the chat, the community and payments.
- Your health and fitness data is used to serve you. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with data brokers.
- We show no ads and use no tracking or advertising tools.
- Your trainer sees what they need to coach you, only while you have chosen them.
- The AI coach receives your data only after you agree, and you can withdraw at any time.
- You can download a copy of your data and delete your account in the app, or ask on our website if you cannot open the app.

## What we collect

Name: your first and last name, if you give them. A trainer's payout details include the account holder's name; an agent's listing shows the shop's name.

Email address: used to sign in, to send you a code when you sign up or reset your password, and to reach you about your account.

Phone number: asked when you create an account. An agent's listing shows the shop's phone number, which the agent chooses to publish.

Account identifiers: your username and your account number.

Shop location of an agent: for agents, the city and area of their shop, shown with their listing.

Date of birth, age and gender: your date of birth is required when you sign up, because the app is for people aged 14 and over. Age and gender help plan training and nutrition.

Health information: height, weight and weight over time, injuries and the injury areas you choose, allergies, and the allergen check of your diet plan. You choose whether to give these; some features need them. Your injury note and injury areas are stored encrypted.

Fitness and training activity: your workouts, sets, weights and repetitions, routines and progress, goals, diet plans, the meals you tick, skip, swap or rate, challenges you join, and summaries of your progress.

Purchase and payment history: the plans, coaching months and programs you pay for, and every movement of money in your account.

Wallet and payout details: your wallet balance; for trainers, the ShamCash address and holder name where withdrawals are paid (stored encrypted) and each withdrawal; for agents, the cash they hold for the platform and its settlements.

Chat messages: the messages, photos, voice notes and videos you exchange with your trainer or your client.

Conversations with the AI coach: what you write to the AI coach and its replies, and the notes it keeps about your training, diet and past chats so it can remember you.

Photos and videos: your profile photo, photos and videos you send in the chat or add to posts and challenges, and a trainer's photos, videos and program covers.

Voice notes: the voice notes you send in the chat.

Posts, comments and other content you create: posts, comments, challenges, likes and follows; for trainers, your bio, specialisations, certifications, exercises, routines, templates, saved meals and programs.

Reports and requests you send us: reports about content or people, and a request to delete an account made on our website.

Identity and qualification documents: for trainers who ask to be verified, the identity document, photo and certificates they upload, and our decision. They are kept in private storage and opened only through links that expire after a few minutes.

Device push token: the token that lets us send notifications to your phone.

App activity: what you do in the app (for example a workout completed, a meal ticked, a badge earned), your app sessions, your settings and consents, and how much you use the AI coach.

IP address and device details: the IP address and the device or browser description sent with some requests, kept with your activity log, your app sessions and the record of wallet actions.

We also keep sign-in codes and password-reset codes, stored hashed, only until they expire.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, your browsing history or payment card numbers. [APP: true while the app sends no location with its session reports; see docs/store/privacy_labels.md.] You never enter a ShamCash password, PIN or code in the app.

## Why we use it

- To provide the service: your account, training programs, workout logging, diet plans, the chat with your trainer, the community, challenges and badges, notifications, the wallet and payments.
- To personalise it: your plans follow your goals, body data, injuries, allergies and the foods you like; the AI coach answers from your data once you allow it.
- To keep it safe and lawful: to prevent fraud and abuse, keep the record of every payment and wallet action, answer reports and enforce these rules, and meet our legal obligations.
- To improve it: to count how the app is used, and, only if you turn on "Help us improve", to keep anonymous copies of AI coach conversations.

## Health information

Health and fitness data is sensitive. We use it only to serve you: to plan and track your training and nutrition, to show it to the trainer you choose, and, if you agree, to let the AI coach answer you. We never use it for advertising or marketing and never sell it. Diet plans are guidance, not medical advice: check with your doctor before changing how you eat or train, especially if you have a health condition or a severe allergy.

## Who receives your data

Your trainer: while you have an approved coaching, your trainer sees the profile they need to coach you (including your health notes and injury areas), your training log, your diet plan and nutrition record, your food preferences and your chat. A trainer who has only received your request sees a short summary without health notes.

Your clients, if you are a trainer: they see your profile, your prices where the app shows them, and the routines, diet plans and messages you write for them.

Other users: what you publish (your username, name and photo, posts, comments, likes, follows, challenges, leaderboard places and badges). Agents: when an agent tops up your wallet, they see your name, your account number and the amount.

## The AI coach

The AI coach reads your data only after you agree. The app asks first, and until you agree the coach does not answer. You can withdraw your agreement at any time in the app; it takes effect with your next message, and you can then delete everything the AI coach keeps about you (its chats, notes and usage records). "Help us improve" is a separate choice, off unless you turn it on: while it is on, anonymous copies of your conversations are kept to improve the coach. They carry no name, account, email, phone number or link, and body data only as ranges. They are deleted when you turn the setting off, when you delete your account, or after at most 365 days. The AI coach can make mistakes and is not a substitute for your trainer or your doctor.

## How long we keep it

- While your account exists, we keep what you need to use the app.
- Activity log and app sessions (with IP address and device details): 180 days.
- Notifications in your inbox: 90 days.
- The AI coach's record of your habits and the daily nutrition tips: 365 days.
- Anonymous AI coach copies: at most 365 days.
- Progress dashboards and answered coaching requests: 180 days.
- Sign-up and password-reset codes: 1 day.
- A verification document a trainer replaced: 90 days after it was replaced.
- A request to delete an account made on our website: 180 days after we handled it.
- Payment, wallet and coaching records: kept after your account is deleted, attached to the anonymised account, because the law and the other people involved in a payment rely on them. A withdrawal keeps the payout details it was sent to. [LAWYER: confirm the period and the obligation.]
